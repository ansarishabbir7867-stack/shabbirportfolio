Tetra Pak Design

A three-flavour juice carton range for Fruva.

Fruva is a three-flavour juice carton range built on one system: a bold wordmark, a 100% Natural seal, a flavour illustration with a juice splash, and three icon callouts (Natural Goodness, Rich in Vitamin C, No Added Sugar). Back and side panels carry nutrition facts, ingredients, FSSAI certification and recycling information. Colour is the only thing that changes between flavours, so the range reads as one family on the shelf.

Sustainable Luxury Candle Brand Packaging Design

Suvasa, a five-scent soy wax candle range.

Suvasa is a five-scent soy wax candle range with sustainability at its centre. Each box carries a hand-painted pattern drawn from its own ingredients (roses and pine, lemon and lavender, khus and mogra, sandalwood and vanilla, cinnamon and neroli), so a scent is recognisable before the box is opened. One shared layout keeps the range cohesive: a round Suvasa label on the front, and a frosted panel on the back for ingredients, safety notes and price.
